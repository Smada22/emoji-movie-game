          How to play
          
  
          • Start a puzzle: emoji #1 appears and a 5-minute clock starts.
            • 
  
          • A new emoji appears every 30 seconds, when you tap “Reveal”, or after each wrong guess.
            • 
  
          • Points = (1000 − 150×(emojis−1)) × (1 − 0.5×secs/300) × hints × 0.85^wrong × streak.
            • 
  
          • Hints: 📅 year ×0.85, 💡 genre+country ×0.75. Streak bonus +10% per prior solve (max +50%).
            • 
  
          • 4 wrong guesses, a skip, or time out = 0 points and the streak resets. One accepted answer per puzzle.
